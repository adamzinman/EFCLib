Option B and three variations

All four use the same data, colors and click behavior: click anywhere on the card to open the loop's settings popup. Values move from a simulated loop, and the popup's mode, SP and OUT controls change them. PIC-205 is in high alarm, so you can see the alarm state.

B1 · ORIGINAL

Bar-graph card (150×156 @1080p)

A vertical PV bar with a blue SP pointer and orange alarm ticks, plus an OUT bar.

B2 · HORIZONTAL

Wide bar card (260×92)

The same idea laid on its side, so it fits in a row beside equipment or above a pipe. The darker band on the bar shows the normal range between the low and high alarms, so drift is obvious before an alarm.

B3 · WITH MINI TREND

Bar card + sparkline (172×176)

Adds a small trend of the last ~40 s of PV (black) against SP (blue dashed), and a deviation chip (PV − SP) that turns orange past 10 units. Operators see whether the loop is settling or oscillating without opening anything.

B4 · SLIM COLUMN

Twin-bar column (104×190)

A narrow card with PV and OUT as side-by-side vertical bars. Several loops fit in a tight row, like a faceplate group or the right-hand panel.
